{# WHAT THE DATA SHOWS #}

What the data shows so far

Key findings from our ongoing sensor deployment and analysis.

Indoor ≠ outdoor

At Belauri, indoor PM₂.₅ concentrations are significantly higher than outdoor during the winter–pre-monsoon haze season, with a median indoor/outdoor ratio above 1.

Data gaps are real

Sensor outages due to power failures and internet outages are common in rural Nepal. Missing data must be measured and reported, not silently discarded.

Sensor reliability varies

Sensor uptime depends heavily on power and connectivity type. Solar and cellular configurations perform differently from grid + Wi-Fi installations.

Coverage gap outside Kathmandu

Most low-cost sensor deployments in Nepal are concentrated in Kathmandu Valley. Terai and hill districts remain largely unmonitored — a critical gap for national-level exposure assessment.
