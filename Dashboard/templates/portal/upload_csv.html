{# Conflict / duplicate policy #}

Duplicate and conflict handling

  • Exact duplicates (same sensor + pollutant + timestamp + value) are silently skipped and counted in "Duplicates".
  • Conflicts (same sensor + pollutant + timestamp but different value): the existing row is kept; the new row is stored with is_duplicate=True and a flag_reason noting the conflict. Both are visible in the admin.
  • Rows with missing timestamps or unrecognised serial numbers are skipped.
  • Quality flags (GOOD / SUSPECT / BAD / MISSING) are automatically assigned based on measurement ranges. You can override them in Django admin.
