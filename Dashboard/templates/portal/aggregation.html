About aggregation

  • Reads from CanonicalReading (GOOD and UNVALIDATED quality only).
  • Writes TenMinAggregate, HourlyAggregate, and DailyAggregate — one row per sensor × window × pollutant.
  • Uses update_or_create — safe to re-run multiple times.
  • Charts on the sensor detail and analysis pages read from these aggregate tables.
  • On Render, aggregation runs automatically every 10 minutes via the nepal-aq-aggregate Cron Job.
  • API: POST /api/v1/aggregate/ with a MAINTAINER/ADMIN key triggers the same computation.
