About Nepal Air Observatory

Open data and analysis on PM₂.₅ exposure, monitoring gaps, and sensor reliability in Nepal.

{# ── Mission ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────── #}

Mission

To provide open, transparent air quality data from Nepal — starting with low-cost sensor deployments, building toward a public research platform that communities, policymakers, and scientists can use.

Approach

We deploy calibrated low-cost sensors at paired indoor/outdoor sites, ingest and validate the data openly, and publish both the raw data and the cleaning methodology — so anyone can verify or extend our work.

{# ── Current deployments ──────────────────────────────────────────────── #}

Current deployments

Belauri, Kanchanpur District

28.6844°N, 80.3646°E · Terai region

Active

Paired indoor/outdoor deployment: Bluesky 81432434001 (outdoor) and Air Assure 81442406076 (indoor). Data collection began July 2025. The site is designed to study the indoor/outdoor PM₂.₅ relationship during Nepal's winter–pre-monsoon haze season (November–March).
